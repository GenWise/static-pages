Our founders

Shrikant, Vishnu and Rajesh founded GenWise in 2017 to work with gifted students year-round.

Shrikant Patil

LinkedIn

Shrikant is responsible for GenWise's strategy and growth, and leads Gifted Lab as well as sales and marketing. He was an early investor in and director of Educational Initiatives (Ei), and with Vishnu he started the Ei ASSET Summer Program in 2015 when Duke TIP left India. Before Ei, he spent 15 years at Intel in Israel, the US and Asia. He has engineering degrees from the College of Engineering, Pune and the University of Colorado, Boulder.

Vishnu Agnihotri

LinkedIn

Vishnu leads GenWise's curriculum and teacher-development work. He built and runs GenWise's network of expert mentors, who work on improving the teacher-student interface across every GenWise programme: My Misconception Mentor, Godrej Tomorrow Makers, the GenWise Summer Programme and Gifted Lab. Before GenWise, he worked in educational assessment at Educational Initiatives (Ei) and was Academic Director of the Ei ASSET Summer Program (2015–2017), India's leading residential programme for gifted students. He has an engineering degree from IIT Madras.

Rajesh Panchanathan

LinkedIn

Rajesh runs GenWise's operations and technology initiatives. He follows Generative AI closely and built the in-house tools GenWise runs on. He also leads GenWise's work with a leading Indian publisher, enabling several thousand college students to evaluate their relationship and skills with AI. Before GenWise, he spent his career at Accenture, where he became a Partner in 2007 and also led HR for its technology delivery centres across Asia-Pacific. He has engineering degrees from IIT Madras and Caltech and an MBA from IIM Lucknow.
