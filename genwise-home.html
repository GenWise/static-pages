An experience like no other

The LSS Shield

Everything we build into the GenWise Summer Program sits on three things — Learning, Social and Safety. Residential, on the MAHE Manipal campus.

Learning

Our world-class mentors and thoughtfully designed courses ignite curiosity and passion in your child. From academic excellence to creative exploration, we provide a learning experience that goes beyond textbooks.

Social

Learning extends beyond the classroom. Our Residential Coaches create a supportive community, helping children navigate the program and build lifelong friendships. From finding their tribe to trying something new, your child blossoms in a space where they feel truly at home.

Safety

Your child's safety is our top priority. We enforce strict safety protocols — no child is ever alone, dormitories are impeccably maintained, and our residential campus meets the highest standards of comfort and security.

Our work in gifted education

Nearly two decades of it, in India

Gifted education in India has a short institutional history. GenWise's team has been in it since the beginning — first inside Duke's program, then building the replacement for it.

2008–2015

Duke TIP in India

Duke TIP ran gifted summer programs in India, using the Ei ASSET Talent Search above-level test to identify students for the program.

2016–2017

Ei ASSET Summer Program

When Duke TIP exited India, Shrikant Patil and Vishnu Agnihotri launched a replacement. Northwestern CTD, ATDP Berkeley and Purdue GER2I recognised the test as an entry point to their own programs.

2017–2021

GenWise is founded

Shrikant Patil, Vishnu Agnihotri and Rajesh Panchanathan founded GenWise to work with gifted students year-round, not only over a summer.

2021 onwards

Gifted World

Ei, GenWise and Northwestern CTD came together to form a community for gifted students, parents and educators. GenWise carries the work in India.
