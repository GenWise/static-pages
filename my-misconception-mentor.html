The Hidden Challenge in Every Classroom

Students appear to understand concepts in class, solve practice problems correctly, yet consistently make the same fundamental errors. This isn't a teaching failure—it's a misconception challenge that requires targeted intervention.

80%+ of Students

harbour persistent misconceptions in key Math and Science concepts, even after traditional instruction. The Ei ASSET diagnostic data reveals these hidden gaps across thousands of classrooms.

MATH

Fraction Operations

The Misconception:

Students think "5 upon 6" and "5 sixths" are different concepts, leading to confusion in operations and word problems.

"Our vocabulary and use of words matter… wrong vocabulary shatters concept building and creates anxiety."

— Teacher, Shiv Nadar School

MATH

Place Value Understanding

The Misconception:

Only 50% of Grade 3 students correctly identify tens and ones when presented in word format, despite mastering digit recognition.

25% of students incorrectly identify "30 tens and 6 ones" for the number 236, showing procedural learning without conceptual understanding.

SCIENCE

States of Matter

The Misconception:

Students memorize properties of solids, liquids, and gases but fail to understand particle behavior and transitions between states.

Students can list properties but struggle with real-world applications like why ice floats or how evaporation works at room temperature.

MATH

Mathematical Procedures

The Misconception:

Students rely on memorized rules without understanding the underlying logic, making them fragile when faced with novel problems.

"If you give out rules and formulas, children forget concepts. But if you have a process of questioning, learning becomes fundamental."

— Teacher, GEMS Modern Academy

What is My Misconception Mentor?

'My Misconception Mentor' is an online teacher development offering that helps teachers to tackle the root causes of major student misconceptions and common errors identified by ASSET, India's leading diagnostic assessment. It is offered in 4 series- Primary Math, Middle School Math, Primary Science/EVS, and Middle School Science.

Each series has 8 two-hour online sessions. Teachers work on practical assignments in-between the sessions and are provided feedback on their submissions. There are also 16 optional 'Teacher Hour' sessions that run through the year in which subject teaching experts are available twice a month, to respond to teacher queries live. Teachers can share specific doubts about concepts, ask for teaching ideas and resources or anything pertinent to student learning and assessment. Thus teachers receive support well beyond the end of the course.

The program has been received very positively by leading schools in India and the UAE. The course approach and topics covered are available in the detailed sections below.

Why the Program Works

The program focuses on addressing root causes of misconceptions rather than superficial fixes, helping teachers build deeper subject and pedagogical content knowledge in the process that leads to sustained enhancement of teaching practice. Teacher motivation to enhance their practice is built because of the practical action-based approach in the program that provides early wins. There is a balance between reflection, theory and practical application.

My Misconception Mentor - Program Structure

Based on feedback from teachers, we have enhanced the course structure to allow deeper engagement with the content.

PRIMARY

Foundation Level

The Primary Math and Science Foundation courses will continue to run in one 8-session series each.

MIDDLE SCHOOL

Multi-Track Structure

Middle School Math:

The Middle school Math course will be offered as two independent 8-session series - Track A and Track B. The two tracks will cover different topics. One track will be offered at a time.

Middle School Science:

The Middle school Science course will consist of three tracks running simultaneously- Physics/Chemistry/Biology. Each will be an 8-session course and a teacher who registers will choose to attend any one.

Why This Structure?

  • Permit a better & deeper coverage of the extensive content in Middle school Math and Science
  • Allow teachers more time to engage with the course and take the learnings to class
