About GenWise

GenWise is an education company that supports gifted children to achieve their potential. Three founders with backgrounds in student assessment, technology and management consulting started it in 2017. We run India's only residential enrichment programme for gifted students, and mentor gifted students year-round online. We also work with schools and their teachers in helping remove student misconceptions. We believe AI holds great potential — we work with thousands of college students to become comfortable building out their careers in this era, and with hundreds of school teachers in exploring how AI tools can enhance their productivity and their teaching and learning outcomes.

What we do

GenWise Summer Programme

India's only residential enrichment programme for gifted students, held each May at MAHE Manipal and each June at MAHE Bengaluru. About one in ten students attends on a scholarship.

Gifted Lab

Year-round online enrichment for advanced learners in Grades 5–8, taken from home or through their school. Most bright students are pushed to go faster; Gifted Lab goes deeper.

Godrej Tomorrow Makers

We are the STEM partner on the Godrej Foundation's talent platform, which aims to support a few thousand gifted students from less-advantaged backgrounds.

My Misconception Mentor

We help Maths and Science teachers in a dozen premium schools in India and the UAE find and fix their pupils' misconceptions.

GenAI for educators and students

We have trained 300+ educators at Shiv Nadar, Sri Kumaran, GEMS Dubai and other schools on Generative AI. This quarter we launch an AI literacy course for several thousand college students, under the banner of one of India's largest national newspapers.
