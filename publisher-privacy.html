GenWise Publisher: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026. GenWise Publisher is operated by GenWise TalentDev Private Limited (“GenWise”), Bengaluru, India. Contact: privacy@genwise.in.

This policy covers one tool, GenWise Publisher. It does not cover GenWise's programmes, website forms or payments.

What this tool is

GenWise Publisher is an internal tool used only by the GenWise team. It publishes GenWise's own posts to GenWise's own Instagram, LinkedIn and YouTube accounts and reads how those posts perform. It is not a public app: there is no public sign-up, and nobody outside the GenWise team can use it. Every post needs the approval of two GenWise team members before it is published.

What the tool holds

  • Our own accounts' statistics. After a GenWise team member authorises it, the tool can publish posts to GenWise's accounts and read their statistics, such as views, likes, comments, followers and reach.
  • Comments on our posts. The tool reads comments left on GenWise posts so the team can see which need a reply. It may hold the comment text and the commenter's public username for that purpose only. This is the only data the tool holds about people outside GenWise, and we delete it on request.
  • Link clicks. Links in our posts may pass through a GenWise link that counts clicks per day. We store the counts only, not who clicked, IP addresses or device identifiers.
  • Sign-up counts. We store how many people signed up for a programme, by day and by how they heard about us. No names, phone numbers or other contact details are held in this tool.

Children and consent

GenWise works with school students, so we take particular care with posts that show children.

  • A post that shows or names a child is published only after a parent or guardian has given consent, and the tool checks that consent again at the moment of publishing.
  • The tool holds only an anonymous consent reference and its status (active or withdrawn). It does not hold children's names or contact details.
  • Photos and videos are kept only for the time it takes to publish a post, and are then deleted from the tool.
  • A parent or guardian can withdraw consent at any time by writing to privacy@genwise.in. We will then stop publishing the affected posts and remove the ones already published from GenWise's accounts.

What the tool does not do

  • It does not sell, rent or share data with anyone, and does not use it for advertising.
  • It does not read the accounts, profiles or messages of anyone other than GenWise.

Where data is kept and how it is protected

Data is held in a database account owned by GenWise, and only the GenWise team can access it. Access tokens for Instagram, LinkedIn and YouTube are kept in an encrypted secrets store. They are never shown to team members or written to logs.

How long we keep data

Statistics and counts are kept for as long as GenWise uses them to review how its posts perform. Access tokens are kept only until they expire or are revoked. Comment text is deleted once it is no longer needed for a reply, or sooner on request.

Google, YouTube, LinkedIn and Meta data

When the tool accesses YouTube, it uses the YouTube API Services, and by using it you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. The tool's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We use data from Google APIs only to publish GenWise's own videos and show GenWise's own channel statistics. We do not transfer it to others, use it for advertising, or allow people to read it, except as needed to run the tool for GenWise. Data from LinkedIn and Meta is used only to publish GenWise posts and read GenWise's own statistics, as their API terms allow.

Revoking access and deleting data

A GenWise team member can revoke the tool's access at any time: from Google account connections for YouTube, the Apps and websites settings on Instagram, and the Permitted Services settings on LinkedIn. Anyone can ask us to delete data we hold about them by writing to privacy@genwise.in. We will reply within 30 days.

Your rights under Indian law

GenWise follows the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and its rules as they apply to us. Where we hold personal data about you or your child, you can ask to see it, correct it or have it erased, and withdraw consent, by writing to privacy@genwise.in. If you are not satisfied with our reply, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
