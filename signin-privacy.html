GenWise Sign-in: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026. Operated by GenWise TalentDev Private Limited (“GenWise”), Bengaluru, India. Contact: privacy@genwise.in.

This policy covers only the sign-in to GenWise's private pages, such as our staff portal and the dashboards we share with partner schools. It does not cover GenWise's programmes, website forms or payments.

What sign-in is for

Some GenWise pages are open only to named people: GenWise staff and the people at partner schools and organisations we work with. Sign-in checks that you are one of them. There is no public sign-up.

What we receive

You can sign in with Google or with a one-time code sent to your email. Either way, we receive:

  • your email address, which we check against the list of people allowed to see that page;
  • with Google, also your name and profile picture, which Google shares by default. We do not use them.

We do not receive your Google password, and we do not get access to your Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google data.

What we keep

  • A sign-in record: your email address, the page, the time, and whether you were let in. We use it to help people who cannot get in and to check that only the right people did.
  • A cookie in your browser that keeps you signed in, for up to one month.

Sign-in runs on Cloudflare Access, which processes these details for us under its own privacy terms.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent or share your details, and do not use them for advertising.
  • We do not send you email because you signed in, apart from the one-time code you ask for.

Google data

Our use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We use it only to confirm who is signing in.

Removing access and deleting data

You can remove GenWise from your Google account at any time under Third-party apps and services. To have your email taken off a page's list, or to ask what we hold about you, write to privacy@genwise.in. We will reply within 30 days.

Your rights under Indian law

GenWise follows the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and its rules as they apply to us. You can ask to see, correct or erase personal data we hold about you, and withdraw consent, by writing to privacy@genwise.in. If you are not satisfied with our reply, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
